GBV Safeguarding Controls

Each control maps to a specific way a standard video conferencing system could expose or re-traumatise a survivor. These are engineering requirements, not preferences.

01

Recording disabled at policy level

Consultation rooms have recording, transcription and cloud storage features disabled centrally — not just unticked locally — so no counsellor or admin can accidentally create a permanent record of a disclosure.

02

No auto-framing or face tracking

Consultation rooms use fixed wide-angle lenses only. The system never actively hunts for and zooms onto a survivor's face. PTZ auto-framing is reserved for boardrooms only.

03

Physical camera privacy shutters

Every consultation room camera has a manual sliding cover, giving the survivor and counsellor a visible, non-digital guarantee that the camera is off — important when trust in "the app says it's off" is not enough.

04

Point-to-point encrypted calls only

Consultation rooms connect counsellor-to-survivor directly, end-to-end encrypted, never through a multi-party cloud bridge that a third party could be added to without either party knowing.

05

Soundproofing and acoustic isolation

Acoustic panels and sealed doors stop conversations bleeding into corridors or adjoining offices — a physical AV specification item, not just a software control.

06

Local admit-only join

Every consultation room call requires the in-room counsellor to admit each participant individually. No waiting-room bypass, no link-sharing that lets an unvetted person drop in.

07

HQ remote access disabled by default

Even the HQ admin (you) cannot view or join a consultation room session without break-glass authentication, which is automatically logged and timestamped in the central audit trail.

08

Content-blind audit logging

HQ logs who joined which room and when — for accountability and safety — but never logs or stores call audio or video content. The log proves access happened without creating a record of what was said.

Net effect: the Boardroom tier optimises for normal collaboration — clarity, scheduling ease, screen share. The Private Consultation Room tier optimises for the opposite: minimal data capture, maximal local control, and a paper-trail-free conversation. For this organisation, that privacy guarantee is the actual product being delivered.
